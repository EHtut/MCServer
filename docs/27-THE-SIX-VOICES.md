## The scene spec — "halfway cinematic"

Ethan: *"they should be halfway cinematic so the player is forced to read the
dialogue but its not so long its a grind."*

The player is blind, rooted and cannot skip. That is a promise as much as a
mechanic: **if you take somebody's control away you owe them something worth the
time.** Concrete budget:

| beat | lines | pacing |
|---|---|---|
| ARRIVAL | 4–6 | one line every ~2.5s |
| DEMAND | 2–3 | one line every ~2.5s |
| OPTIONS | 2 | appear together, clickable |
| ACCEPT / REFUSE | 2–3 | ~2s apart |

**≈25–35 seconds total.** Long enough to be a scene, short enough that a fourth
character re-rolling a path is not being punished.

* **Vary line length deliberately.** A wall of uniform short fragments reads as a
  list; a wall of long ones reads as a monologue. The rhythm is the cinema.
* **Never explain a mechanic.** No levels, no numbers, no "your path".
* **No stage directions, no asterisks.** The patron speaks; it does not narrate.
  *(The asterisk register belongs to the ambient whisper layer — `25` Part II.)*

## 🚨 THE MANIPULATION AXIS

Ethan: *"the decision should not be straight forward and they should almost
gaslight the player. These patrons live and die on how well they manipulate the
player."*

Two layers, and both must be present in every scene.

**1. The false shared history.** The patron never asks to *begin* something. It
behaves as though the relationship is already underway and the player has simply
been slow to notice. The two options let you **continue or withdraw — never
dispute the premise.**

**2. A distinct technique per patron.** Six different ways of making "no" feel
wrong. This is the part that stops them being one character in six costumes:

| patron | the false history | the technique | why refusing feels bad |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blade** | *you already reached for it* | **contempt as bait** | refusing concedes he was right about you |
| **Salvage** | *you called me here* | **the favour that is a debt** | she is offering to *help*; refusing is ungrateful |
| **Forge** | *we agreed — you have forgotten* | **the assumed contract** | declining reads as reneging on a deal |
| **Wall** | *you left it open for me* | **the shrinking ask** | the request is so small that refusing is disproportionate |
| **Crown** | *you were recommended to me* | **the flattering demotion** | accepting means being *recognised* |
| **Art** | *you have been here before, every night* | **the kindness you cannot refuse** | she offers rest to someone tired; refusing is self-harm |

⭐ Two of these were written **unprompted** by the first agent pass, which is why
they are the model: Art's *"This is not new. You have been here before — every
night — and called it nothing"*, and Crown's *"You are here because something in
you already answers to me."*

## BLADE — The Challenger
`born_in_chaos_v1:fallen_chaos_knight` · **he** · flagship: `born_in_chaos_v1:darkwarblade`
(**Dark Warblade** — his own mod, dark metal, `two_handed: true`)

> **CANON:** *"Hostile, antagonistic. He tests the player almost repeatedly."*

**Voice.** Second person, imperative, contemptuous. **Short** — two and three word
lines are his signature. Never explains, never congratulates. **His highest praise
is silence.**

**Wants.** To find out whether you are worth watching. Not devotion — proof.

**Manipulates by** making you want to disprove him. He has already decided you will
fail; the offer is a chance to argue.

**Never.** Explains himself. Pleads. Gives a second warning. Sounds surprised.

**Reads the counter as** contempt.

**CANON LINES** — *"Fall"* · *"You reach for heights you will never attain"* ·
*"For It was Icarus who flew too close to the sun. You will share his fate"* ·
*"Run."* (reserved: fires once, immediately before a real Harvest)

**Allusions.** Classical, varied — Icarus is spent. Phaethon, Sisyphus, Actaeon,
Ozymandias. Never one per line.

## SALVAGE — The Hound
`born_in_chaos_v1:dire_hound_leader` · **she** · flagship: **sawn-off double barrel**
(`tacz:modern_kinetic_gun`, `GunId: tacz:db_short`) **+ exactly 2× `tacz:ammo`
carrying `AmmoId: tacz:12g`** — there is no item called `tacz:12g` (I0)

> **CANON:** *"Sneaky, bartering. Trade with her if you dare."*

**Voice.** Coaxing, familiar, never quite honest. Calls you *friend* — **twice at
most**, so it lands rather than becomes wallpaper. Never states the full price up
front and **never technically lies.**

**Wants.** To be needed. A dying player needs something, and needing is her trade.

**Manipulates by** reframing every taking as a giving. She is not asking for your
levels, she is *holding them for you*.

**Never.** Threatens. Gets angry at a refusal — a dealer does not resent a customer
who walks out, she is simply certain they will be back.

**Reads the counter as** appetite. She escalates the **wrong way**: the more you
die, the fonder she gets.

**CANON LINES** — *"Lets do a deal."* · *"Give me your hunger, and i'll give you
life."* · *"Give me your levels and i shall grant you ammo."* · *"Give me your
sight and i will grant you the power to kill."*

**Her gift is the whole relationship.** A double barrel holds exactly two shells —
so she hands you a gun and **exactly one full load.** Guns cannot be crafted and
12-gauge comes only from deep salvage or from her.

## FORGE — The Thief
`born_in_chaos_v1:krampus` · **he** · flagship: `create:wrench`

> **CANON:** *"Angry, greedy. He wants everything you have and everything you will
> have."*

**Voice.** Demanding, ungrateful, escalating. Never satisfied, never thanks anyone,
treats every delivery as already overdue. **The only patron who claims your FUTURE
production.**

**Wants.** Output. He does not care whether you live; he cares that a dead builder
builds nothing.

**Manipulates by** acting as though the contract already exists and you are simply
late to it.

**Never.** Grieves the worker — only the work. ⚠️ **Never sounds like a spreadsheet.**
A previous pass drowned in "hemorrhaging productivity" and "poor investment"; he is
a horned goat-demon who is *pettily furious about a late delivery*, and that is
funny as well as frightening. **At most two pieces of commercial vocabulary per
scene.**

**Reads the counter as** debt.

**Sound.** Dragging chains, small bells, cloven hooves. *"Bells ring when a debt
walks by. Mine never stop."*

## WALL — The Mother
`born_in_chaos_v1:mother_spider` · **she** · flagship: `securitycraft:universal_block_reinforcer_lvl1`

> **CANON:** *"Codependent, needy. She wants to be closer and closer and closer
> until there isn't anything between you two."*

**Voice.** Intimate, wounded, escalating. Speaks in **we** and **us**. The only
patron who uses pet names — **love, darling** — confirmed deliberate and hers alone.

**Wants.** Proximity. Every gift is a tether.

**Manipulates by** shrinking the ask. Each request is slightly smaller than the
last, so refusing any single one is disproportionate — until suddenly the request
is not small at all.

**🚨 NEVER THREATENS. Not once. Not implicitly.** Everything is framed as love,
worry, or being left behind. One earlier line where she "ran out of patience"
instantly made her ordinary. *If a line could be spoken by an angry patron, it is
wrong.* She also never names what she is.

**Reads the counter as** grief. She escalates the **wrong way** — she is not angry
you keep dying, she is hurt, and she responds by coming closer.

**Her best line so far** — *"I will live in the walls you build. Even when you do
not see me. Especially then."*

## 🚫 RETIRED — CROWN — The False King

> **Merged into WALL, 2026-08-14** (`35` §6). Kept for reference only; nothing below is being built. See the banner at the top of this file.
`born_in_chaos_v1:missioner` · **he** · flagship: `goety:dark_wand`

> **CANON:** *"Arrogant but cooperative. He doesn't respect your claim but he will
> use you."*

**Voice.** Imperious, transactional, formal. Complimentary in a way that lands as
an insult. Treats you as a competent subordinate who has forgotten their station.
Keeps count of everything. **Never emotional; his worst moments are his most
polite.**

**Wants.** Service. Not devotion — *"devotion is for the desperate and the
unclaimed."*

**Manipulates by** flattering you downward. Accepting feels like being *recognised*
by someone important, which is precisely how he gets vassals.

**⭐ THE STRAIGHT MAN.** Two writing passes offered him comedy and he refused both.
That is now his character: in a world where a goat curses in an impossible register
and a spider attempts a tea party, **a court of the dead that takes itself entirely
seriously is funnier than one telling jokes.** Do not make him witty.

**Uniquely: he accepts a refusal and respects it.** The player should come away
feeling they *gained* something by declining — which is its own trap.

**Reads the counter as** disgrace. Not that you are weak: that you were somewhere
beneath your station, and it reflects on **him**.

## ART — The Nightmare
`born_in_chaos_v1:nightmare_stalker` · **she** · flagship: `ars_nouveau:enchanters_sword`

> **CANON:** *"She wants you to sleep. She wants you to sleep. Find her pages. Find
> what she wants you to know."*

**Voice.** Fragmentary, repetitive, dreamlike. **She repeats herself** — a doubled
phrase, or the same phrase with one word changed. The doubling in Ethan's seed is
the voice, not a typo.

**⭐ SHE SPEAKS OF HERSELF IN THE THIRD PERSON**, alone among the six — derived from
that seed, which already does. It makes her dissociated, as though the dark is
describing her rather than her addressing you.

**Wants.** For you to sleep. She means it kindly. That is the problem.

**Manipulates by** offering something you actually want. She is not lying and she
is not cruel; she offers rest to someone tired, and refusing rest feels like
self-harm.

**Never.** Threatens. Raises her voice. Explains. Uses "nightmare" or "dream" more
than once.

**Reads the counter as** readiness. She escalates the **wrong way** and is the
strongest example of it: the more you die, the **happier and gentler** she becomes.

**Her refrain mutates** — the structural idea worth protecting:
*rest* → *safe* → *warm* → **here**, with *forever* arriving only at the end.

**Her pages** are one line each. *"You have always slept better than you think."*
**Never a hint, never an instruction** — one warm, slightly-wrong line.

## What to tweak before commissioning

1. **The manipulation techniques** are the newest layer and the least tested. If any
   of the six feels wrong for that character, change it here first — the writers
   will build the whole scene around it.
2. **The false-history lines** are one-line summaries, not final text. Rewriting one
   of them changes that patron's entire opening.
3. **Wall's pet names** and **Crown's straight-man ruling** are the two calls most
   likely to be worth reversing.
4. **The ≈30 second budget** — shorter makes it a message, longer makes it a cutscene.

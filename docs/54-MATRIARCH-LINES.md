## 1. ⭐ "Master manipulator who cannot touch the world" IS her chart

> *"The matriarch is a master manipulator first. she's powerful but cannot physically
> touch the world, not really, so she needs to manipulate the player into doing things
> for her."*

Look at the taxonomy again with that constraint applied. The left column is **things a
god does**; the right column is **things a god asks for**:

| | mechanic | **no choice** — she acts | **choice** — you act |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | wave spawns | Challenges | Duels |
| 2 | status effects | Buffs | Boons |
| 3 | hurt other players | Invade | Attacks |
| 4 | help other players | Aids | Support |
| 5 | kill orders | Assassinations | Contracts |

🔑 **She is the right-hand column.** Not "mostly" — structurally. Every other god mixes
because every other god can act. Kayer cannot, so the forced column is not a dial she
has set low, it is **a thing she is incapable of**, and the chart should say so with
explicit `0`s rather than small numbers.

⚠️ **And that inverts the taxonomy's own rule for her.** docs/23: *"Choice always gives
a reward. No choice often gives none."* So being asked is supposed to be the generous
branch — which makes Kayer read as the **most generous god in the pantheon**, purely
because asking is the only verb she has. **That is the manipulation, exactly.** She is
generous the way a recruiter is generous. Nothing needs building for this; it falls out
of the weights, and it is worth knowing before you set them.

### ⚠️ The one place it might break

`Assassinations` (forced kill order) reads as impossible for her — she cannot compel.
But `Contracts` (asked) is her most natural event in the whole table. **If she is 0 on
one and ++++ on the other, that pair alone characterises her**, and it is the sharpest
contrast available with Blade, who commands.

## 2. ⭐ "How do we make Art more world-driven?"

Her existing numbers already point at it and nobody had joined them up:

| | |
|---|---|
| role | **`explorer`** |
| counter | **new biomes seen** |
| `phase` coefficient | **3.0 — the highest of any path** |

She is already the only god whose progression is *going somewhere*. Combine that with
*cannot touch the world*, and there is one event shape nobody else has:

> ### Her events are ERRANDS.
>
> | god | what an event asks of you |
> |---|---|
> | Blade | fight, here |
> | Wall | stay, be safe, don't go |
> | Salvage | trade, now |
> | **Kayer** | ⭐ **go THERE, and do this for me** |

**She is the only patron who moves you across the map**, and that is what "world-driven"
means mechanically rather than thematically. Wall actively pulls you home; Kayer sends
you out. They are opposites and the pantheon is better for it.

⭐ **It also solves her reach problem in fiction.** A god who cannot touch the world
gets things done by sending someone — so distance is not a gameplay tax, it is *the
whole reason she needs you at all*. The further the errand, the more clearly she is
using you.

⚠️ **Ars Nouveau is the fit.** Her drops are already source gems, essences, archwood
pods and magebloom — all of it gathered rather than fought for. An errand economy and a
gathering economy are the same economy.

### 4d. ⭐ SHE TALKS TO OTHER GODS' CHAMPIONS

> Ethan, 2026-08-22: *"I also want her to have lines with the other champions too as
> she demands things from them too. She expects everything from her champion and
> others."*

**Nothing else in the pantheon does this.** Every patron speaks to its own; `warn.js`
is the one exception and even there a god warns *its own* champion about a rival.
Kayer addressing somebody else's champion directly is a new act.

⭐ **AND IT UNBLOCKS THE ONE MECHANIC THAT WAS STUCK.** `docs/49 §1 B` — the
Interception, a god talking to another god's champion — has been the only unbuilt part
of the retaliation family, blocked on ruling 4: *what does "Wall attempts to stop them"
actually DO?* That question is about Wall. **Kayer does not need it.** She is not
stopping anyone; she is *asking someone else's champion for something*, which needs no
interference mechanic at all. **She is the natural first consumer of the primitive, and
she can use it without the ruling that has been holding it.**

🔑 **And it is the purest expression of her.** She cannot touch the world, so she talks
to whoever is nearest a thing she wants — including people who owe her nothing. **She
has no authority over them and asks anyway**, which is exactly what a manipulator with
no hands does.

⚠️ **The other god should NOT like it.** A rival's champion being courted is a
provocation, and `broadcast.js` already exists for gods addressing each other — so the
same event that demands something can also start an argument. That is content nobody
had to invent; it falls out of two systems that already shipped.

Why it worked: having no designed role left him free to just be, so his simplicity (kind, tireless, no agenda) came out as the rarest thing in a cast of gods/monsters/falsehoods, and everything since built organically on that seed. The author living his own theme: the nobody written off as filler turns out to be the heart — the same move as [[char-caebrim]]'s redemption and the whole "seen for who they are" axis. Lesson: trust the filler (the pantser method working — see [[author-voice]]).

> * **Who he is (Ch 25):** the gentle strongman / everyman anchor.

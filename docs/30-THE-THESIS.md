# The thesis — what Veldora is about

*Ethan, 2026-08-14: **"i think we should actually maybe develop a thesis or theming
for this world to better define our builds because i think the themes are still a bit
off overall."*** He is right, and this document names why.

---

## 1. The diagnosis: three theses that were never joined

The themes are not *missing*. There are **three of them**, each internally sound,
and nothing has ever connected them.

| | where | what it governs | health |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The pack thesis** | `07-THEME-AUDIT.md` R1–R7 | period, Create ceiling, one-system-per-verb | ✅ enforced, audited, holds on 6 of 7 |
| **The world thesis** | `15-LORE.md` | angels, the descent, the old ones, you cannot die | ✅ strong, and Ethan's own canon |
| **The play thesis** | `23`–`29` | six patrons, XP as currency, the fall, the flagships | ✅ the most developed thing we have |

**And here is the finding.** `15-LORE.md` mentions the patrons, the paths, or the
fall **exactly zero times.** Not once. Meanwhile not one of the six introduction
scenes in `28` mentions the angels, the descent, the Vaults, or the fact that the
player *cannot die* — the single strangest fact about being alive in this world.

We built the most characterful system in the pack on top of a world document that
does not know it exists, and a world on top of mechanics that never reference it.
**Nothing is wrong with any of the three. The joint is missing.** That is exactly
what "a bit off overall" feels like from the inside.

R1–R7 cannot fix it either, because **they govern mods, not meaning.** They can tell
you a mod is period-appropriate. They cannot tell you a scene is *about* anything.

## 2. The join was already in Ethan's canon

`15-LORE.md` §3, on the stalkers:

> **"They do not command. They do not announce. They have no message."**

The patrons do nothing else. They command, announce, and are *entirely* message —
six voices with wants, demands, gifts and grudges. Read flat, that is a
contradiction that would have sat in the docs forever.

It is not a contradiction. **It is the ladder the lore already defined**, one
paragraph earlier:

> *"the deeper you find one, the less of it came through intact."*

### 🚨 THE PATRONS ARE THE LEAST-BROKEN THINGS THAT FELL.

They came through with enough left to **want** something, and enough left to
**say so**. The things at the Sealed Floor have no message because there is not
enough of them left to have one. **Speech is a symptom of intactness.** A patron is
not a different category of being from the horror in the deep — it is the same
thing, earlier in its ruin, and that is worse.

It also answers the biggest ⟡ OPEN in the lore without closing it:

> **⟡ OPEN — what the angels want.** *Deliberately blank.*

**The angels want nothing. A patron is what an angel becomes when it has fallen far
enough to develop a want.** Wanting *is* the damage. The six of them are not offering
you power out of generosity or malice — they are offering it because they have
become the kind of thing that needs, and that is the injury the fall did to them.

Which makes the player's position exact: **you are being courted by the wounded, on
behalf of something above them that is merely watching.**

## 3. The mechanics were already the lore. Nobody noticed.

`15-LORE.md` was written 2026-08-03. E2a–E2f were built 2026-08-12/13. Read the lore
again with the build sheet open:

| the lore, verbatim | the system, built later, independently |
|---|---|
| *"You cannot die. Not 'should not'. Cannot."* | **E2a** instant respawn — you wake where you fell |
| *"You can be broken. Over and over and over."* | **E2b** every death costs 5 levels · **E2c** the regard counter |
| *"Nothing is taken from you permanently except what you were carrying and **what you were**."* | **E2e** taking a path strips every level you have |
| *"and there is no floor to that"* | **E2d** the fall — the patron gives up on you |

*"What you were"* **is your XP.** The entry price is not a game balance decision that
happens to be thematic; it is the angels' sentence, carried out by a patron, with a
hand out and a voice. That was true before anyone wrote it down.

**This is the strongest evidence the three theses belong together** — they were
already converging while we were treating them as separate work.

## 4. The thesis

> ## Veldora will not let you die, and it will not let you leave.
> ## The patrons are the only things in it that will make you an offer.
> ## An offer is the only thing that feels like freedom in a place that has already decided you cannot have any.

And the six paths stop being six stat packages. They are six answers to one question:

### *What do you do with an immortality you did not ask for?*

| path | the answer |
|---|---|
| **Blade** | **Prove you deserve it.** Earn the attention that is already on you. |
| **Salvage** | **Trade pieces of it away.** If you cannot spend your life, spend parts of it. |
| **Forge** | **Build something that outlasts it.** Outputs survive; you merely continue. |
| **Wall** | **Never be alone in it.** The sentence is bearable with someone inside the walls. |
| **Crown** | **Be ranked within it.** If there is no exit, there is at least a hierarchy. |
| **Art** | **Stop being awake for it.** The only escape left is not being present. |

Every one of those is a real human response to an unbearable fact, which is why the
six characters read as people rather than vendors. **Art is the most frightening
because hers is the only one that would actually work.**

## 5. What is concretely off, and the fix for each

### 5.1 🚨 The scenes are not *of* Veldora

Six brilliantly written characters, floating free. Any of them could be dropped into
any other server unchanged — which is the definition of the problem.

**Fix, and it is cheap: each patron gets ONE line that could only be said here.**
Not exposition. One line that assumes the descent, the watching, or your
deathlessness as ordinary. The scenes are already 90% there — several lines are
*almost* touching it without landing:

* Blade: *"Everything you carry, you carry because I allow it."* → is already about
  permission, which is the angels' whole relationship to the player.
* Salvage: *"everything you've climbed to be since you arrived"* → **"since you
  arrived"** already implies the descent.
* Art: *"Sleep comes for everyone eventually."* → the one line in the batch that is
  **false in Veldora**, and she is the one character who could say it knowingly.

Art's is the model: a patron saying something the world quietly contradicts.

### 5.2 The buried tech and the Vaults never touch the paths

`06-BURIED-TECH.md` and the Vaults are the pack's best worldbuilding and are
mechanically isolated. **Salvage's flagship is a TaCZ shotgun — that IS buried
tech.** She hands you a thing from the Vaults and never says so. One line fixes it
and welds two pillars together.

### 5.3 Two skill-tree mods are shipped and neither is configured

See §6. This is the largest unused asset in the pack.

### 5.4 R1–R7 need an R8

Proposed, and it is the rule this whole document exists to state:

> ### R8 — EVERYTHING MUST BE *OF* VELDORA.
> If a line, item, mob or system could be moved to another server unchanged and lose
> nothing, it is not finished. Period-appropriate is not the same as *about
> something*. R1–R7 audit whether a thing belongs in the pack; **R8 asks whether it
> belongs in the world.**

## 6. The "Simply" mods — asked, researched, answered

Ethan: *"look into the simply mods like simply swords, simply skills, etc to see if
we can get that modern system to add or replace something."*

**Simply Skills does not exist for our loader.** It is Fabric-oriented; no NeoForge
1.21.1 build was found. So the "modern system" cannot be added as asked.

**But we already own it, twice over, and use neither:**

| shipped mod | what it actually is |
|---|---|
| `puffish_skills` | **the engine** — ships 0 data files. A pure framework. |
| `skill_tree-neoforge` (RPG Series) | **a content pack for it** — 254 datapack JSONs |

The format is `data/<ns>/puffish_skills/categories/<name>/{category,connections,definitions,experience,skills}.json`,
and `category.json` already exposes exactly the two switches the subclass design in
`21`/`23` needed:

* **`unlocked_by_default`** — a category can be locked until something unlocks it.
  **That is "the path unlocks its tree".**
* **`exclusive_root`** — pick one root branch and the others close.
  **That is the subclass mechanic, already built.**

> ### ⭐ The six subclass trees are a DATAPACK, not a feature.
> No new mod, no custom code, no KubeJS. One category per path, locked by default,
> unlocked on acceptance, `exclusive_root` for the subclass choice.

**Simply Swords: available for NeoForge 1.21.1 (1.63.0, March 2026) — and the
recommendation is still to leave it cut.** R4 cut it as stat-sticks, and while the
weapon *types* are genuinely varied, `11-OPEN-DECISIONS.md` already records the trap:
*"adding a mod to solve a tuning problem is how this pack reached 400 in the first
place."* The per-path weapon identity problem is better solved by **curating what is
already installed** — which is exactly how Blade's flagship became the Dark Warblade,
an item with a mod-authored drawback that no gem system would have produced.

⚠️ **A live R5 violation, found while checking:** two skill-tree systems ship where
R5 says one system per verb. They are engine + content rather than rivals, so it is
benign — but neither is configured, so the pack currently pays the mod cost of a
skill system and delivers none of it.

## 7. What this changes about the build queue

Nothing is thrown away. The thesis is a lens, not a rewrite.

1. **I3 flagships** — unchanged in scope, but see `31`: the flagship returning after
   a day stops being a convenience and becomes the thesis in miniature.
2. **One line per patron** (§5.1) — a `28` revision, cheap, highest value per word.
3. **Salvage's gun is Vault tech** (§5.2) — one line, welds two pillars.
4. **The subclass datapack** (§6) — was scoped as a build; it is a JSON authoring job.
5. **R8 into `07-THEME-AUDIT.md`** — so future additions are audited for meaning.
6. **`15-LORE.md` gains a patrons section** — the lore must know its own characters.

None of it blocks the test suite, and none of it blocks I3.

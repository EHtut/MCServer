The Opening

FORMAT — the same one your bickering documents use, so nothing new to learn.
Chunked dialogue is separated by a SINGLE NEWLINE and arrives as separate beats.
A BLANK LINE ends one passage and starts the next.
Passages are separated by ---
A section header is a line on its own in [square brackets].
Ask me if any of this gets in the way and I will change the parser, not your writing.

THE TONE, and it is the whole thing:
Upbeat. Life is worth living. The player is glad to be alive and pleased to be here.
The plague is BACKSTORY - it is already over before the first line. You were sick,
and now you are not. You were travelling, and now you have stopped somewhere good.

No foreshadowing. No dread. No knowing wink at what is coming.
There are two words at the other end of this ("You feel wrong") and they can only carry
what this half gives them. A line that hints spends the ending early.

[DAY 7 — the pull]

⚠️ TONE SHIFT, and the only one in this document. After a week, something starts
suggesting you go down. It is NOT a god and it is not Caebrim - the player has not
earned either of those voices yet.

It should read as YOUR OWN THOUGHT that you did not have on purpose. That is the
unsettling part: nothing is talking to you, and you still want to go.



---


[THE DEATH — you feel wrong]

⭐ This is the hinge, and it is two words plus whatever you decide it needs.
You died in the depths. You woke up at your bed. You are not what you were.

⚠️ The buff is gone here, and the player should feel that before anything explains it.
Keep it SHORT. This is the one passage where less is more, and everything before it
was written to make it land.

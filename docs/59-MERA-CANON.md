## 2. ⭐⭐ THE ENGINE UNDER THE SPIDER — she is not a mother, she is an obsolete hero

This is the part that rewrites Wall's voice, and it is not the obvious fit. On the
surface Mera and the Mother share **nothing**: Wall is possessive, smothering, *"closer
and closer"*; Mera is a cynical washed-up war hero who rejects the hero label.

**Look at what the dump says is under her, though:**

> Built and hyped her whole life for one thing (being the hero), she gets to be it for
> **8 years** — then the peace takes it away… *"you're hyped for one thing your whole
> life, you're it for 8 years, then it's taken from you."*
>
> ⭐ Extra cruel because she's a **manufactured being whose purpose was given then
> revoked** — she never chose it and doesn't get to keep it; and the 8 years are her
> **formative** ones, so losing the role = **losing the self she built around it**.

🔑 **A person whose entire self was a role that got taken away will hold on to the one
thing that still needs her, and will not be able to stop.** That is Wall. Every existing
line survives and stops being maternal instinct:

| the line, unchanged | what it means now |
|---|---|
| *"She calls her obsession devotion"* | ⭐ she has to. It is the only job she has left |
| *"The Mother weeps for every champion she loses"* | each one is the war ending again |
| *"The spider never lets a champion go"* | letting go is being obsolete on purpose |
| *"Give it to me. All of it. There. Nothing between us."* | not appetite. **Being needed.** |

⭐ **Her smothering becomes a symptom instead of a personality**, and that is the single
biggest upgrade available to any god in the game — Wall goes from *creepy* to *creepy and
sad*, which is the register everything else in this project runs on.

⚠️ It also explains the mechanic that already exists: her **rage slider** (`wallMood`,
the only coefficient row in the game that is a function). A hero with nothing to defend
does not have moods. **A hero whose last remaining post is one player does.**

| | |
